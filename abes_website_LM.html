NEWS About ABES

ABES Engineering College (ABESEC) is a premier private engineering institution located in Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR, established in 2000. Widely recognized for its structured environment and stellar hiring outcomes, the college has officially transitioned to an autonomous status under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU).
If you are looking for specific portals or details about the campus, here is the complete overview:

Key Institutional Anchors

  • Engineering Wing (College Code 032): You can manage admissions, view B.Tech cutoffs, and track institutional curriculum details directly via the Official ABESEC Portal.
  • Business School (College Code 1193): The dedicated portal for postgraduate management programs, including industry-aligned MBA curriculums, can be accessed through the ABES Business School Hub.
  • ABESIT Group of Institutions: The sister institution situated nearby at Crossings Republik, focusing on emerging tech programs, is reachable at the ABESIT Home Page.

Latest Placement Data & Admissions (2026 Batch)

  • Placement Highlights: The comprehensive Placement 2026 Report records an impressive 80.06% placement rate. The outgoing batch successfully locked in 1,870 total job offers, featuring a highest package of ₹45 LPA and solid placement tracks across major tech firms like Infosys.
  • Admission Pathways: B.Tech admissions for the 2026–27 session are primarily processed through JEE Main 2026 ranks and the formal UPTAC Counselling window. Alternatively, management quota pathways are open for direct admissions.
  • Academic Regime: The campus features highly rated academic labs and smart classrooms, maintaining a professional culture that strictly enforces a 75% mandatory attendance policy.
